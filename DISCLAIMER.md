# Disclaimer

## Not legal advice

This software produces a structured regulatory analysis. It is **not legal advice**
and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Regulatory compliance depends on
facts that are not visible on packaging artwork — production methods, certifications,
laboratory results, corporate registrations, and measured dimensions among them.

Have final artwork reviewed by a qualified regulatory consultant or attorney before
commercial printing.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by the U.S. Food and

Protection. References to their regulations are for identification only.

## Regulation currency

Rules are built from Code of Federal Regulations text retrieved from eCFR. The eCFR
is updated continuously, and this repository is not. Verify any citation against the
current text before relying on it.

## Known limitations

- **Measurement.** Type sizes, panel areas, and rule weights cannot be determined
  from a photograph. These resolve to NEEDS-INFO by design.
- **External facts.** Certification status, directory listings, laboratory results,
  and production methods are not visible on a label and must be supplied.
- **Interpretation.** Some regulations are internally inconsistent. 21 CFR 101.93(d)
  contains a symbol-linking clause and an each-panel clause that pull against each
  other; the rule library flags this rather than asserting a single reading.
- **Scope.** Labeling compliance only. Facility registration, Prior Notice, state law
  such as California Proposition 65, and cGMP obligations are out of scope.
- **Jurisdiction.** United States only.
